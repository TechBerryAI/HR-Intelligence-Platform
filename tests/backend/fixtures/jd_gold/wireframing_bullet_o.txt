Job Description: Wireframing and Figma Design - Mumbai

We are seeking a skilled Wireframing and Figma Designer to join our team and help us create
intuitive and visually appealing user interfaces. The ideal candidate is a creative problem-
solver with a strong understanding of user experience (UX) principles and a proficiency in
Figma.

Experience: 2+ years

Key Responsibilities:
• Wireframing: o Create low-fidelity and high-fidelity wireframes to visualize the structure and layout of digital products.
• o Collaborate with UX researchers and designers to understand user needs and preferences.
• o Iterate on wireframes based on feedback and testing.
• Figma Design: o Design visually appealing and user-friendly interfaces in Figma.
• o Ensure consistency with brand guidelines and design standards.
• o Create interactive prototypes for testing and validation.

Required Skills:
o Proficiency in Figma and other design tools
o Excellent communication and collaboration skills
o Problem-solving and critical thinking abilities
o Prototyping

Education
Bachelor's degree preferred.
