Job Description: Video Editor

We are seeking a creative and detail-oriented Video Editor to join our team.
The ideal candidate will have a strong understanding of video production,
including editing, motion graphics, and sound design.

Location: Vikhroli, Mumbai

Key Responsibilities:
• work closely with our production team to create engaging videos for various platforms, including social media, marketing, and internal communications.

• Ensure video content is optimized for different platforms (e.g., social media, website, YouTube, etc.).
• Review and edit videos based on feedback, ensuring high-quality standards are met.
• Manage and organize video files and assets for easy retrieval and editing.
• Keep up with industry trends and best practices in video editing, and incorporate new techniques as appropriate.

Education
Proven experience as a video editor or similar role.
Proficiency in video editing software such as Adobe Premiere Pro, Final
